# Decycle.io

**Decentralized Recycling Infrastructure**

Decycle enables scaling of recycling infrastructure through blockchain technology by decentralizing deployment, maintenance, and operations. The platform creates a closed-loop recycling economy where smart cans, powered by blockchain and AI, automatically sort materials. To drive participation, Decycle transforms recycling into an engaging game where users build virtual sanctuaries that directly fund real-world environmental initiatives, creating a seamless connection between everyday recycling and global impact.

(3 screenshots showing dapp flow)

Decycle has created a novel "recycle-to-earn" mechanism where both physical recycling actions and virtual participation combine to drive measurable environmental change. Community members can deploy smart cans, users earn rewards by recycling, and everyone contributes to real environmental initiatives.

**What it does:** Decycle leverages blockchain technology to create a decentralized network of AI-powered smart recycling cans that can be deployed, maintained, and operated by community members. The system automates sorting, tracks environmental impact, and gamifies the recycling experience to increase participation while ensuring transparent distribution of rewards.

**Why it matters:** Traditional recycling infrastructure is centralized, expensive to scale, and lacks proper maintenance. Decycle's decentralized approach allows for rapid expansion of recycling infrastructure with built-in economic incentives for proper maintenance and operation. This solves the critical scaling problem in waste management while creating measurable environmental impact through transparent blockchain tracking.

**What's Possible:** Community-driven deployment of recycling infrastructure without centralized coordination, transparent tracking of environmental impact on the blockchain, decentralized maintenance and operations of smart cans, gamified participation through virtual sanctuaries with four environmental categories (Flora, Fauna, Aqua, Sustainability), collection of Eco-Guardian NFTs representing endangered species, and direct funding of environmental initiatives with verifiable impact.
